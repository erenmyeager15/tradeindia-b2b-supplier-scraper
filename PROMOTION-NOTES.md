# TradeIndia Supplier Scraper Promotion Notes

## Positioning

- Public TradeIndia supplier and product listings by keyword.
- Best first demo: `led light`, `maxResults: 1`, proxy off.
- Useful fields to show: supplier name, business type, city, state, price display, MOQ, trusted seller flag, product URL, supplier profile URL, and scraped timestamp.
- Good audience: sourcing teams, exporters, wholesalers, procurement analysts, market researchers, and B2B operators comparing public supplier listings.

## Short Video Outline

1. Search `led light` with `maxResults: 1`.
2. Show the dataset table with product, supplier, city/state, price, MOQ, and URLs.
3. Export to CSV or Excel.
4. Mention cost controls: one keyword first, one result first, proxy off unless needed, maximum cost per run if needed.

## LinkedIn Draft

I polished my TradeIndia Supplier Scraper on Apify for quick B2B supplier research.

It searches public TradeIndia listings by keyword and exports structured rows with product name, supplier name, business type, city/state, price, MOQ, trust flags, website/profile URLs, product URLs, and timestamps.

The default example is intentionally small: `led light`, 1 result, proxy off. Good for checking the output before scaling a sourcing or market-research workflow.

## Reddit / Discord Draft

I updated a TradeIndia scraper for public supplier/product listings.

It can export keyword search results with supplier name, product name, business type, location, price/MOQ, trust flags, and source URLs. The default run is only one result with proxy disabled, so it is easy to inspect the output before increasing limits.

Useful for B2B sourcing research or comparing public supplier listings. It does not extract hidden emails, phone numbers, or private contact data.

## Do Not Claim

- Do not claim access to private TradeIndia dashboards, inboxes, leads, or customer data.
- Do not claim hidden phone/email extraction.
- Do not present it as a guaranteed verified supplier database.
- Do not promise anti-bot bypassing or unlimited scraping.
- Do not promote heavy runs without first showing cost controls and responsible-use limits.
